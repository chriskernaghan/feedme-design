Jo runs the data studies on feedme.design. That means choosing a fixed panel of shops, measuring the same thing in the same way on each one, recording when it was done and publishing the method alongside the results, so that others can check the work or repeat it.

Jo's focus is on questions a shop owner can act on, such as where delivery costs first appear or whether guest checkout is offered, and on being straightforward about what a sample can and can't show.
