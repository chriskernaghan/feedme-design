Alex edits feedme.design and writes most of its breakdowns, which follow a single flow in a real shop from first click to payment and record what helps and what gets in the way. Each one notes the device, country and date it was checked on and includes dated screenshots, so readers can retrace the steps and decide for themselves whether the conclusions hold.

Alex also writes the shorter insight pieces, where the aim is to make one clear argument and link every claim to its source.
