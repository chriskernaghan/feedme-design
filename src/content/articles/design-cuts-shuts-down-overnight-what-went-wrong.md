> **Archive note:** This is a newly researched replacement for an unavailable Feed Me Design article. It was prepared on 13 November 2026 and is not a recovered copy of the original.

Design Cuts closed in 2025, leaving customers looking for ways to retrieve purchases. In a [notice dated 25 January 2025](https://bokettori.com/blog/news/design-cuts-closure-how-to-access-your-purchases), asset creator Bokettori addressed buyers who might have lost access to its products. [Loveleigh Loops’ course resource page](https://loveleighloops.teachable.com/courses/design-elements-in-procreate/lectures/13199429) also notes that the closure made a resource bundle unavailable.

Those accounts establish a practical access problem. They do not establish why the business closed. The surviving URL uses the earlier headline’s wording, but we do not have enough evidence to describe the exact notice period or assign a financial cause.

## Our view

For a customer, the transaction may feel complete as soon as the payment clears. The ability to retrieve the file can still depend on a service they do not control.

A download library is convenient until access disappears. This matters for a designer who needs to reopen a client project months later. Having an invoice is useful evidence of a purchase, but it does not replace the missing file.

The lesson for a digital shop is to make the delivery model clear. Explain whether customers should keep their own copies and how long account access is offered. Avoid making promises about permanent availability that the service cannot support.

Closure planning belongs in product design because it affects a task the customer may reasonably expect to complete. It should include an understandable notice and a workable export route where possible.

## What to do about it

If you sell digital files, test the journey from purchase to a usable local copy. Make the relevant licence easy to save with the file.

If you buy them, keep a backed-up copy with its purchase record and licence. For a missing Design Cuts purchase, check the original creator’s own guidance; do not assume every creator can restore access.
